Condominium & HOA Board Representation

Specialized legal counsel for New York condominium boards and homeowner associations

Experienced Board Counsel

Condominium board and HOA representation is a highly specialized field requiring not just legal expertise but practical understanding of community governance, property management, and the unique challenges boards face. Ward & Rafter currently represents several condominium boards and homeowner associations throughout Staten Island and New York, providing superior representation and delivering the results our clients need.

Our experience matters. We understand the Condominium Act, we know how boards operate, and we've navigated the complex legal issues that arise in community association governance. When issues arise, and they will, you want experienced counsel who can resolve them efficiently and effectively.

Comprehensive Board Services

Bylaw Enforcement & Amendments

Bylaws are the foundation of community governance. We assist boards with enforcing existing bylaws and amending them when necessary to address evolving community needs. From architectural restrictions to pet policies to rental restrictions, we help boards maintain standards while ensuring compliance with New York law.

Unit Owner Disputes & Collections

When unit owners fail to pay common charges or violate community rules, boards need effective legal action. We represent boards in collection proceedings, lien foreclosures, and enforcement actions. Our approach is firm but measured, designed to bring unit owners into compliance while protecting the board's interests.

Board Governance & Elections

Proper governance is critical to a well-functioning community. We advise boards on procedural requirements, meeting protocols, voting procedures, and election processes. When disputes arise over board elections or procedural issues, we provide clear guidance and, when necessary, litigation support.

Construction Defect Claims

Construction defects can create significant liability for condominium associations. We represent boards in pursuing claims against developers, contractors, and design professionals for building defects, water infiltration, structural issues, and other construction-related problems.

Vendor & Management Company Contracts

Boards enter into numerous contracts with property managers, contractors, vendors, and service providers. We review and negotiate these agreements to protect the association's interests and ensure favorable terms. When disputes arise with vendors or management companies, we provide strategic counsel and litigation support.

Common Element Maintenance & Disputes

Questions about common element maintenance, repairs, and improvements frequently arise. We advise boards on their maintenance obligations, special assessment procedures, and disputes with unit owners over maintenance responsibilities.

Strategic Counsel for Complex Issues

Board representation isn't just about handling problems as they arise. It is about preventing problems before they start. We provide strategic advice on:

Insurance coverage: Reviewing policies to ensure proper coverage for the association and unit owners.

Rental restrictions: Implementing and enforcing restrictions on unit rentals, including short-term rentals like Airbnb.

Reasonable accommodations: Navigating requests for reasonable accommodations under fair housing laws.

Board member liability: Advising on directors' and officers' liability issues and insurance coverage.

Litigation Support

When disputes can't be resolved through negotiation, litigation may be necessary. Our attorneys have extensive experience representing condominium boards in all types of litigation:

• Collection proceedings and lien foreclosures
• Enforcement actions against unit owners
• Construction defect litigation
• Contract disputes with vendors and management companies
• Disputes with neighboring properties
• Insurance coverage disputes
• Challenges to board actions

We represent boards in Richmond County courts and throughout New York State, with a track record of successful outcomes.

Confidentiality & Discretion

Board matters often involve sensitive issues such as financial problems, neighbor disputes, and contentious enforcement actions. We understand the importance of confidentiality and discretion. Our advice is always provided with an eye toward maintaining community harmony while protecting the association's legal rights.

Why Boards Choose Ward & Rafter

Specialized Expertise: Condominium and HOA law is a niche practice area. We focus on this work and understand the nuances.

Practical Solutions: We provide advice that is legally sound and practically implementable. Boards don't have unlimited budgets, and we deliver value.

Responsive Service: Board members are volunteers serving their communities. We respect their time and provide timely, accessible counsel.

Litigation Experience: When enforcement or litigation is necessary, we have the trial experience to achieve results.

Long-Term Relationships: We view board representation as a long-term partnership. We get to know your community, anticipate issues, and help you govern effectively.

Serving Your Community

Every condominium and homeowner association is unique, with its own character, challenges, and priorities. Ward & Rafter provides tailored legal counsel that fits your community's needs.

Whether you need ongoing board counsel, assistance with a specific project, or representation in litigation, we're here to help. Experience matters. Let us worry, so you don't have to.
