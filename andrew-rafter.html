Andrew Rafter

Partner

Trial attorney and litigator with deep experience in complex civil litigation, real estate disputes, and environmental law

Overview

Andrew Rafter is a founding partner of Ward & Rafter, LLP and the firm's lead litigator. With over 15 years of legal experience across state and federal courts, Andy brings a rare combination of courtroom skill, analytical depth, and subject matter expertise to every matter he handles. His practice is centered on complex civil litigation, real estate disputes, and environmental law — areas where the stakes are high, the legal issues are nuanced, and results matter.

Andy's approach is methodical and client-centered. He invests the time to understand not just the legal dimensions of a dispute, but the practical realities and objectives that drive his clients' decisions. That combination — rigorous legal analysis paired with a genuine understanding of what clients need — is what sets his representation apart.

Complex Civil Litigation

Andy's litigation experience spans the full arc of civil disputes — from initial investigation and pleadings through discovery, motion practice, trial, and appeal. For eight years before founding Ward & Rafter, he honed his litigation skills at a busy and well-respected general practice firm on Staten Island, handling a broad range of complex civil matters in New York Supreme Court, federal district court, and the Appellate Division and Appellate Term of the Second Department.

His litigation work has encompassed commercial disputes, breach of contract, injunctive relief, condominium and HOA enforcement actions, employment discrimination, and personal injury matters on both the plaintiff and defense sides. Andy is equally at home writing appellate briefs and arguing before courts as he is in the trial courtroom — a versatility that gives clients a consistent and experienced advocate at every stage of a case.

In 2014, Andy authored an amicus brief in Capruso v. Village of Kings Point, 23 N.Y.3d 631, a landmark case in which the New York Court of Appeals made new law regarding the Parkland Alienation Doctrine. The Court quoted directly from Andy's brief in articulating its reasoning — a rare recognition reserved for the most persuasive and carefully reasoned advocacy. That achievement reflects Andy's ability to engage complex legal questions at the highest level and contribute meaningfully to the development of New York law.

Real Estate Litigation

Real estate disputes are among the most consequential legal matters a property owner, developer, or community association can face. Andy has litigated a wide range of real estate disputes in New York courts, bringing the same rigor and strategic thinking to property matters that he applies to his broader litigation practice.

His real estate litigation experience includes boundary and encroachment disputes, easement and access rights, title conflicts, breach of contract, specific performance claims, and partition actions. He also handles real estate-related injunctive proceedings — situations where waiting for a full trial is not an option and immediate court intervention is required to protect a client's property rights.

Andy understands that real estate litigation is rarely just about the law. Property disputes involve significant financial stakes, long-standing relationships, and often deeply personal interests. He approaches these matters with the sensitivity and strategic clarity they require, keeping clients informed and in control of their decisions throughout the process.

Environmental Law

Andy's commitment to environmental law predates his legal career. While a student at Brooklyn Law School, he interned at the United States Environmental Protection Agency, Riverkeeper, Inc., and the Kings County District Attorney's Environmental Crimes Unit. This hands-on experience — tracking industrial polluters, pursuing cleanup cost recovery for hazardous contamination, and serving on inter-agency task forces addressing issues such as Jamaica Bay cleanup and environmental legislation — gave Andy a working knowledge of environmental law that is grounded in real practice, not just the classroom.

After law school, Andy co-founded a public interest environmental litigation practice on the East End of Long Island, based in his hometown of Sag Harbor. As part of the Northern Environmental Law Center, he led efforts to halt the illegal sale of public parkland, challenge overdevelopment threatening sensitive ecosystems, and hold local agencies and developers accountable under the law. One of his signature achievements from that period was the firm's successful effort to stop the sale of Fort Pond House in Montauk — a result that demonstrated both his litigation skill and his genuine commitment to conservation.

Today, Andy brings that same depth of knowledge and conviction to his environmental and land use practice at Ward & Rafter. His work in this area includes environmental regulatory matters, land use and zoning disputes, and litigation involving environmental compliance and liability.

Practice Philosophy

Analytical Rigor: Complex cases require careful thinking. Andy invests the time upfront to understand the legal landscape fully before formulating strategy — because the best outcomes come from sound analysis, not improvisation.

Honest Counsel: Andy tells clients what they need to hear, not just what they want to hear. That honesty, delivered with clarity and respect, enables clients to make informed decisions and pursue realistic goals.

Tailored Strategy: No two disputes are the same. Andy tailors his approach to the specific facts, objectives, and practical realities of each matter — whether that means aggressive litigation, a negotiated resolution, or something in between.

Full-Service Representation: From the first consultation through final judgment or settlement, Andy provides consistent, hands-on representation. Clients are never passed off to junior staff on matters that require experienced judgment.
