Landlord-Tenant Law

Representing landlords with rent-stabilized buildings throughout Staten Island and New York. We handle the full spectrum of Housing Court proceedings including holdovers for lease violations, unauthorized occupants, and owner-use; nonpayment proceedings; illegal subletting and unauthorized occupancy; succession rights disputes; and vacancy deregulation matters.

Extensive experience in Richmond County Housing Court with deep knowledge of Staten Island's rental market and rent-stabilized compliance requirements. We protect landlord rights while navigating complex regulatory frameworks.
