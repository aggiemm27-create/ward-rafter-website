Areas of focus

Landlord-Tenant Law

Representing landlords with rent-stabilized buildings throughout Staten Island and New York. We handle the full spectrum of Housing Court proceedings including holdovers for lease violations, unauthorized occupants, and owner-use; nonpayment proceedings; illegal subletting and unauthorized occupancy; succession rights disputes; and vacancy deregulation matters.

Extensive experience in Richmond County Housing Court with deep knowledge of Staten Island's rental market and rent-stabilized compliance requirements. We protect landlord rights while navigating complex regulatory frameworks.

Learn more →

Condominium & Homeowner Association Boards

Specialized counsel for condominium boards and homeowner associations throughout Staten Island and New York. We handle bylaw enforcement and amendments, unit owner disputes and collections, board governance and elections, construction defect claims, and contract negotiations with vendors and management companies.

Our deep understanding of the Condominium Act and New York property law ensures your community's interests are protected with precision and discretion.

Learn more →

Complex Litigation & Trial Practice

Trial-tested advocates with extensive courtroom experience throughout New York's legal system. Regular practitioners in Richmond County Housing Court, Richmond County Civil Court, and Richmond County Supreme Court, as well as Supreme Court Appellate Division and federal courts.

We handle high-stakes civil litigation across practice areas including real estate disputes, commercial litigation, and appellate work. Multiple published decisions in the New York Law Journal demonstrate our commitment to achieving precedent-setting results for clients.
