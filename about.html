Who We Are

Ward & Rafter, LLP is a client-centered law firm proudly serving Staten Island and New York State. Founded by Mark A. Ward and Andrew Rafter, our practice is built on a foundation of experienced advocacy, strategic thinking, and unwavering commitment to our clients' interests.

With over 30 years of combined legal experience, our attorneys bring deep courtroom expertise and practical problem-solving to every matter. We have successfully represented clients across every level of New York's court system, from Richmond County Housing Court to the Supreme Court Appellate Division and federal courts.

The firm's origin story is itself a testament to the quality of its attorneys. Mark and Andrew first encountered each other on opposite sides of the courtroom — Mark as Project Director at CAMBA Legal Services, Andy as an associate at Howard File, P.C. Over years of adversarial practice, they developed a mutual respect for each other's skill and work ethic that ultimately led them to found Ward & Rafter together in 2021.

Staten Island Focus

While we serve clients throughout New York State, Staten Island is our home. We have extensive experience in Richmond County's courts and deep knowledge of the local legal landscape. Our attorneys are regular practitioners in Richmond County Housing Court, Richmond County Civil Court, and Richmond County Supreme Court, giving us unique insight into the issues that matter most to Staten Island landlords, condominium boards, and business owners.

Our understanding of Staten Island's rental market, real estate challenges, and community dynamics allows us to provide tailored, effective representation that gets results.
